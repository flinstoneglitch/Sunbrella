Sunbrella Support

Need help with Sunbrella? We’re happy to help.

Common fixes

Make sure your device is connected to the internet for Game Center and rewarded ads. If audio is missing, check the in-game speaker button and your device volume. Restarting the app can resolve temporary issues.

Contact us

Email sunbrella.support@example.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem. Please don’t include passwords or payment information.
