Sunbrella Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Sunbrella is a casual game published by Colin Oreilly.

Information we use

Sunbrella does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game Center may process gameplay scores and your Game Center profile according to Apple’s policies.

Advertising

Sunbrella uses Google AdMob rewarded advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, we request permission before using data for tracking. You can manage advertising and privacy choices in your device settings.

Service providers

We use Apple Game Center and Google Mobile Ads to provide leaderboard and rewarded-ad features. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children

Sunbrella is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to: sunbrella.support@example.com
